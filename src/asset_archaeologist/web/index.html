    Settings

    Welcome. Pick the folders where your asset packs are (zips, Unity packages, Blender files or folders of models), then press Start scan. Every model is drawn from five sides and indexed so you, and any AI agent you use, can find it by describing it. Nothing leaves this computer.

    1. Library folders

    Folders that hold your asset packs. Sub-folders are included.

      2. Where things go

      About 230 KB per model (a 1,000-model library is about 230 MB).

      Packs are unpacked here while they render, then deleted. Pick a drive with a few GB free.

      3. Blender

      More processes render faster but use more memory. 2–3 suits most computers.

      4. Scan

      The first scan downloads the search model (~800 MB, once). Rendering takes about 0.6 s per model with 3 Blender processes on a laptop with an NVIDIA GPU; slower without one. You can stop at any time; the next scan continues where it left off. You can search while it runs.
